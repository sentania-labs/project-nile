# Product baseline: v0.6

Scott's November 22, 2026 interactive kickoff specification is the authority.
This is a compact implementation reference, not a replacement specification.
It supersedes the earlier v0.5 PostgreSQL/server/mandatory-Firstmate assumptions.

## Product identity

- Original Pharaoh mechanics and recognizable Egyptian atmosphere, with original art.
- One contiguous, finite, expandable Nile Valley and one authoritative clock.
- Local single-player desktop application: Windows x64, macOS arm64, Linux x64.
- Go owns the local simulation; Godot renders and accepts input.
- SQLite per saved civilization; in-memory simulation; deterministic replay.
- Pharaoh housing tiers, desirability, labor, physical walkers, roads and roadblocks.
- No circular municipal coverage substitution, density zoning, unrelated research tree,
  online accounts, required server, PostgreSQL or deployed lab dependency.
- Named settlements inside the world. Local services stay inside boundaries unless
  an explicit sharing policy permits access; eligible service still travels physically.
- Local inventories and physical regional road/river shipments with reservations,
  loading, unloading, travel time and conservation. No teleported trade.
- Original historical advancement adapted to persistent civilization milestones.
- Production art follows shared repeatable generation, processing, validation,
  import, real Godot capture, review and promotion. Paid credits require a budget.

## Neighborhood sandbox

Found a town along the overland Kingdom Road, or enter a village with a partly grown grain
crop. Extend roads, drag out housing plots, provide water and deliver
food to advance housing. Farms, granaries and bazaars occupy their full tile
footprints. The Go simulation tracks grain in buildings, on the road and in
homes. Clay and farm straw feed a brickworks. Builders and brick deliveries
reach a small mastaba along roads. Overseers show the city’s labor and
finances, and overlays identify gaps in service coverage. Save/load preserves
active trips and construction shifts.

The neighborhood target includes supply loss and recovery, roadblocks for
roaming walkers, original housing dependencies and original walker behavior.
Use the mechanics registry to distinguish the working model from remaining
fidelity work.

## Subsequent increments

1. Start with a free-build Egyptian settlement. The first playable build covers
   construction, connected roads, immigration, wells and the first two housing tiers.
2. Complete the local food loop, service walkers, housing requirements and workforce.
   Extend local production, inundation, storage, trade, finances, hazards, civic
   services, religion and monument construction in playable increments.
3. Match the original building availability and historical advancement as evidence
   permits. Use updated original art with the recognizable Pharaoh camera,
   building silhouettes, density and street-level readability.
4. Once the original sandbox plays well, expand to contiguous territory, separate
   settlements and physical regional shipments. Profile the larger map at that stage.

Target 60 FPS at 1920×1080 on RTX 4070 laptop-class hardware, also inspect 720p,
1440p, 3456×2160 and 3440×1440. No population/world-capacity claim is established.
The AFK scope below supersedes the kickoff deferrals for warfare and campaigns.
Multiplayer, infinite terrain, sophisticated congestion, advanced migration and district editing remain deferred. Boundary
design must eventually support nested districts without exposing overlays in
ordinary play. No future system is to be built merely to demonstrate extensibility.


## AFK acceptance agreed with Scott

Deliver an offline Windows free-build game covering the civilian, religious,
military, monumental and scripted player capabilities of Pharaoh and Cleopatra.
Expose late-game events in the sandbox for direct play. Verify the original
where evidence exists and record remaining uncertainties. Include a locally
bundled original score, remappable pan/zoom/rotation, drag-preview roads that
commit on release, a Kingdom Road, and a quiet original-style city interface.
Test supply failures, recovery and SQLite restore during active jobs and battles.

The campaign stretch goal covers both original mission sequences, their branch
choices, objectives and victory transitions, with original writing and artwork.
A mission counts as playable after a briefing-to-victory test and transition.
A catalog of mission data is research evidence. The Windows handoff includes a
fresh sandbox and mature cities for testing farming, trade, monuments and war.
Regional expansion and player-managed intercity transport follow the sandbox.
